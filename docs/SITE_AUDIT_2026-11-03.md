# Stonebridge Las Vegas Homes: site audit and rebuild

Date: 2026-11-03 · Repo: LetMeHelpYouREALTY/StonebridgeHomes · Live domain: www.stonebridgelasvegashomes.com

## 1. What was wrong (before)

### Critical

| # | Finding | Impact | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Canonical, OG URLs, and schema pointed at **heritagestonebridge.com**, but this repo deploys to **stonebridgelasvegashomes.com** (Vercel project `stonebridge-homes`). | Told Google every page was a duplicate of another site. The domain could not rank for anything. | All URLs derive from `SITE_ORIGIN` in `src/lib/site.ts`. |
| 2 | Sitemap and robots advertised `stonebridge-homes.vercel.app`, and were served at `/sitemap` and `/robots` (no extension), so `/robots.txt` and `/sitemap.xml` were 404. | Crawlers had no sitemap; preview host leaked. | Routes moved to `robots.txt/` and `sitemap.xml/`; 12 URLs with real `lastmod`. |
| 3 | **Fabricated listings** on `/homes` (123 Heritage Way $525,000, etc.) with links to pages that did not exist, and invented stats (847 homes, "$1.25M starting", "10/10 rating", golf course, tennis courts). | Misrepresentation risk; nothing in Heritage has a golf course or tennis courts. | Replaced with live RealScout MLS feed plus three verified example listings with MLS numbers and dates. All figures sourced and dated in `src/lib/data.ts`. |
| 4 | Every image reference was a 404: `/images/logo.png`, OG image, all favicons, all eight PWA icons. `public/` held only the manifest and a service worker. | Broken social cards, no favicon, failed PWA install, Lighthouse best-practices hit. | 6 heroes (WebP + JPEG, two sizes), OG card, logo, favicons, 8 icons generated by `npm run images`. |
| 5 | Service worker precached `/styles/global.css`, which did not exist, so `cache.addAll` rejected and the SW never installed. Cache-first strategy would also have served stale prices. | Dead PWA; stale-content risk. | Network-first SW, `allSettled` precache. |
| 6 | Phone number 702-222-1964 (client email/text line) on the public site. | Violates the one-number-per-site rule. | Single constant `AGENT.phoneDisplay`; see open decision A. |

### High

- No site navigation at all: no header, no menu, four orphan pages. Added sticky header, mobile menu, footer with crawlable links.
- "McCarran Airport" (renamed Harry Reid International in 2021).
- Fair Housing proxies: "families, professionals, and retirees", "excellent-rated schools", "peace of mind" security copy in the dead `route.js` data. Removed. New copy names schools and distances only; the 55+ status is stated with the HOPA basis.
- Only 4 thin pages; none targeted the "people also search for" terms.
- Dead code from two earlier frameworks (Svelte `.svelte` + `route.js`, Next.js `styles/`, `src/hooks/sitemap.js`) and three unrelated projects committed at the repo root.
- Brown 1990s palette, emoji icons as imagery, no typography system.

**Keyword handling for the two unrelated entities.** "Stonebridge Learning Academy" is a preschool chain (Rainbow Blvd, Henderson, Sunset Rd) and "Stonebridge hospitality" is Stonebridge Companies, a Denver hotel operator. The `/stonebridge-las-vegas` page answers both queries honestly as a disambiguation, which is the only way to capture that traffic without misleading anyone. The footer carries a not-affiliated line.

**GEO/AEO.** Every page has a direct-answer FAQ block with `FAQPage` schema, a dated source line under every statistic, and entity schema with stable `@id`s (`#agent`, `#website`, `#stonebridge`). Meta descriptions carry the median price and the as-of date so AI overviews can quote a number with provenance.
